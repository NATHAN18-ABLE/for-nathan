Mapo 마포구

Market snacks, the river and live music

Mapo is where neighborhood markets, the Han River and indie music meet. Open the day with market snacks, walk the riverbank and an old railway turned park, and end at a live show near Hongik University.

Length
About 10 hours
Start
Mangwon Station, Line 6

The day

  1. 10:30

    Mangwon Market

    A neighborhood market of croquettes and fried chicken

    • Time here: 1 hr
    • Cost: ₩5,000–10,000
    Details

    A covered market where locals do their daily shopping. Graze on croquettes, sweet-and-spicy fried chicken and fish cakes, a few coins at a time.

  2. Walk 15 min

    11:45

    Mangwon Hangang Park

    A riverside walk a few minutes from the market

    • Time here: 1 hr
    • Cost: Free
    Details

    Follow the 'Han River' signs from the market past Seoul Battleship Park to the riverbank, and walk along the water.

  3. Bus, 15 min

    13:15

    Lunch in Yeonnam-dong

    Lunch in lanes of small restaurants, old Chinese kitchens among them

    • Time here: 1 hr
    • Cost: ₩10,000–20,000
    Details

    Since a Chinese school moved to neighboring Yeonhui-dong in 1969, ethnic Chinese families have run restaurants here. Try their jjajangmyeon or jjamppong, or pick any of the small places in the lanes.

  4. Walk 5 min

    14:30

    Gyeongui Line Forest Park, Yeonnam section

    A park on an old railway line, nicknamed Yeontral Park

    • Time here: 1 hr
    • Cost: Free
    Details

    The old Gyeongui railway now runs underground, and its tracks above became a long, narrow park. The 1.2 km Yeonnam stretch, lined with cafés, leads to Hongik University Station.

  5. Walk 10 min

    16:00

    Hongdae streets and KT&G Sangsangmadang

    Street performers, indie shops and galleries

    • Time here: 2 hr
    • Cost: Free to browse
    • Busking peaks on weekend evenings
    Details

    The streets around Hongik University, Korea's best-known art school, are full of buskers and dancers. KT&G Sangsangmadang has an indie design shop and a free gallery. Have dinner nearby before the show.

  6. Walk 5 min

    19:00

    A Hongdae live club

    An indie band night in a small club

    • Time here: 2 hr
    • Cost: Tickets ₩20,000–50,000
    • Check the schedule and book
    Details

    Hongdae is the home of Korean indie music. Clubs like Rolling Hall, open for some thirty years, host bands most nights.

Prices are rough ranges per person. Opening hours, prices and pop-ups change, so check before you go. Place photos were gathered by the team during research.
