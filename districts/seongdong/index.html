Seongdong 성동구

Old factories, new workshops

Seongsu was a district of shoe factories and metal shops. Many of those buildings are now cafés, pop-up stores and studios, and the shoemakers are still here. Start in a forest, end by the Han River.

About 9 hours

The day

  1. 11:00

    Seoul Forest

    A morning walk among ponds and forest paths

    • Time here: 1 hr
    • Cost: Free
    Details

    Open the day with an easy walk. Ponds, wide lawns and forest paths, right in the middle of the city.

  2. Walk 15 min

    12:30

    Yeonmujang-gil and Seongsu Yeonbang

    Seongsu's main street of pop-ups and design shops

    • Time here: 2 hr
    • Cost: Lunch ₩15,000–25,000
    Details

    Seongsu's main street, lined with design shops, perfume stores and pop-ups from big brands. Seongsu Yeonbang is an old factory turned into shops and restaurants. Have lunch along the street.

    Pop-ups change every few weeks, so there is always something new.

  3. Walk 10 min

    14:30

    Seongsu Handmade Shoe Street

    Where shoemakers have worked for decades

    • Time here: 1 hr
    • Cost: Free to browse
    • Classes are paid
    Details

    Around exits 1 and 2 of Seongsu Station, hundreds of shoemakers have worked for decades. Browse the shared showrooms under the elevated tracks, or join a class at the Handmade Shoe Hope Platform.

    Class times change, so check before you go.

  4. Walk 5 min

    15:30

    A café in an old factory

    A big bakery café in a former factory

    • Time here: 1 hr 30 min
    • Cost: ₩10,000–20,000
    • Weekend queues
    Details

    Seongsu is known for large bakery cafés in former factories. Try Onion Seongsu, once a metal works, or The House of Terarosa Seoul. Rest with a signature pastry and a drink.

    Weekend queues can take 20 minutes or more.

  5. Walk 15 min

    18:00

    Seongsu pork galbi alley

    Marinated pork ribs grilled at outdoor tables

    • Time here: 1 hr 30 min
    • Cost: ₩20,000–30,000
    Details

    Near Ttukseom Station, old galbi houses grill marinated pork ribs at outdoor tables. Daesung Galbi is the best known.

  6. Bus or Line 7, 15 min · or walk 35 min

    19:30

    Ttukseom Hangang Park at night

    Walk the river as the bridges light up

    • Time here: 1 hr
    • Cost: Free
    Details

    Walk off dinner by the Han River and watch the bridges light up. The main riverside area is right outside Jayang Station on Line 7.

Prices are rough ranges per person. Opening hours, prices and pop-ups change, so check before you go. Place photos were gathered by the team during research.
